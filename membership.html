Member benefits, explained.

Member discounts

Receive 5% off every purchase, every day. Over the course of a year, most members save far more than the cost of their membership.

Voting rights

Have a say in how your co-op is run. Vote for the Board of Directors and on major policy decisions that affect the co-op's future.

Patronage dividends

When the co-op has a profitable year, a portion of profits may be returned to members as a patronage dividend based on how much they shopped.

Special sales

Get access to member-only sales and promotions throughout the year, including special discounts on local products.

Membership Policies & About KNFC

Membership Policies

Effective July 1, 2011

Standard Annual Membership: $25/year. Household Membership: $35/year (includes a maximum of 2 adults and any children under 18 who share meals on a regular basis). Lifetime Membership Option: $250 one-time payment.

Members receive 5% off all normal purchases at the store. Student Annual Membership: $15/year — offered to students of any college or university and includes the same standard discount privileges and ordering benefits.

Members may make special pre-orders of bulk or case quantities. For such orders, Members pay only 25% above wholesale cost (pre-paid deposit required). Non-members receive a 10% discount off retail price for pre-orders.

About KNFC & Volunteering

We are a community-owned store. Purchasing a membership not only entitles you to a discount at the register, but makes you a part owner in the Co-op.

Volunteering is appreciated, though not required. Members receive an additional 2% discount for each hour logged in a month, up to 25% off, applied for the entire following month. Operational decisions are made by our Board with input from volunteer committees; members are encouraged to attend meetings and participate.

All meeting times/locations and copies of our by-laws are posted at the store. Receive volunteer hours for attending meetings and participating on committees or the board — ask a staffperson for details or to become a member.
