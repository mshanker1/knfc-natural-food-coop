What we carry.

KNFC offers natural, organic, and locally-sourced products across the store:

  • Fresh produce: Local and organic fruits and vegetables
  • Bulk foods: Grains, nuts, seeds, spices, and more — by the scoop
  • Dairy & alternatives: Milk, cheese, yogurt, and plant-based options
  • Groceries: Pantry staples, snacks, and specialty items
  • Frozen foods: Healthy frozen meals and ingredients
  • Supplements: Vitamins, minerals, and herbal products
  • Body care: Natural soaps, lotions, and personal-care items
  • Household: Eco-friendly cleaning products
