Your Community-Owned
Natural Foods Store

Kent Natural Foods Co-op (KNFC) in Kent, Ohio, is a community-owned grocery store operating since 1972 that specializes in organic, local, and natural foods. Everyone is welcome to shop - membership has its benefits!

What We Offer

Local produce, bulk foods, natural groceries, supplements, eco-friendly products, and more!

Why Shop at a Co-op?

Community Owned

As a member-owned cooperative, profits stay in our community. Your purchases support local farmers and producers.

Quality Products

We carefully select products that meet high standards for nutrition, sustainability, and ethical sourcing.

Everyone Welcome

You don't have to be a member to shop! Everyone can access our quality products and friendly service.

Member Benefits

Members receive discounts, voting rights, and the satisfaction of co-owning their grocery store.
