— Our tagline, on every tote —

"Local. Before local was cool."

Half a century before grocery chains discovered the word "local," neighbors in Kent pooled their grocery money to open a store that bought from farms within driving distance. That's still the whole idea. Apples from down the road. Maple from Geauga County. Greens from the next township over. Coffee roasted a few miles east. We tell you who grew it, who made it, and how far it had to travel — because that part matters.

— The member-owners of KNFC, est. 1971

— Cooperative principles —

Why a co-op, not just a store.

Open to all

You don't have to be a member to shop. Everyone gets the same shelves, the same staff, the same hours.

One vote

Every member-owner has the same vote — regardless of how much they spend or how long they've been around.

Owned by us

No outside shareholders. Profits stay with the people who shop here and the farms we buy from.

Independent

We make our own buying calls. Nobody's regional manager is choosing what's on our shelves.
