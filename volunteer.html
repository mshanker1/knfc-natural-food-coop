Volunteer with us.

Lend a few hours, learn the store, meet your neighbors, and earn discounts on what you take home.

Why volunteer?

Volunteering at KNFC is one of the best ways to connect with the community, learn about natural foods, and help keep our co-op thriving. Our volunteers are essential to our operation, and we truly appreciate every hour of service.

Volunteer benefits

  • Discounts: [X]% off your purchases for every [X] hours volunteered
  • Community: Meet like-minded people who care about good food
  • Learning: Gain knowledge about natural foods, nutrition, and retail
  • Flexibility: Choose shifts that work with your schedule
  • Impact: Help strengthen a community-owned business

Who can volunteer?

We welcome volunteers of all backgrounds and experience levels. Students, retirees, stay-at-home parents, working professionals looking to give back — there's a place for you at KNFC.

How to get started.

01. Contact us

Reach out to our Volunteer Coordinator to express your interest and discuss available opportunities.

02. Apply

Complete a volunteer application. It helps us learn about your interests and availability.

03. Orient

Attend a brief orientation to learn about the co-op, food safety, and your volunteer role.

04. Show up

Begin your shifts and start making a difference in your community.
