Volunteer With Us

Make a difference in your community while earning discounts

Why Volunteer?

Volunteering at KNFC is a wonderful way to connect with your community, learn about natural foods, and help keep our co-op thriving. Our volunteers are essential to our operation, and we truly appreciate every hour of service.

Volunteer Benefits

  • Discounts: Earn [X]% off your purchases for every [X] hours volunteered
  • Community: Meet like-minded people who care about healthy food
  • Learning: Gain knowledge about natural foods, nutrition, and retail
  • Flexibility: Choose shifts that work with your schedule
  • Impact: Help strengthen a community-owned business

Who Can Volunteer?

We welcome volunteers of all backgrounds and experience levels. Whether you're a student, retiree, stay-at-home parent, or working professional looking to give back, there's a place for you at KNFC.

Requirements

  • Must be at least [X] years old
  • Reliable and punctual
  • Friendly and customer-focused
  • Willing to follow food safety guidelines
  • Able to commit to [X] hours per month minimum

Volunteer Opportunities

Stocking & Receiving

Help keep our shelves full and organized. Tasks include:

  • Unloading deliveries
  • Stocking shelves and coolers
  • Rotating products (first in, first out)
  • Checking expiration dates

Time commitment: [X-X hours/week]

Best for: Those who enjoy physical activity

Cashier Support

Assist at the register and help customers. Tasks include:

  • Bagging groceries
  • Helping customers find products
  • Answering basic questions
  • Keeping the checkout area tidy

Time commitment: [X-X hours/week]

Best for: Friendly, outgoing people

Bulk Section

Maintain our popular bulk foods area. Tasks include:

  • Refilling bulk bins
  • Cleaning and sanitizing containers
  • Labeling and pricing
  • Keeping the area organized

Time commitment: [X-X hours/week]

Best for: Detail-oriented individuals

Produce Helper

Help maintain our fresh produce section. Tasks include:

  • Stocking fresh produce
  • Culling and quality checking
  • Misting vegetables
  • Creating attractive displays

Time commitment: [X-X hours/week]

Best for: Early birds (morning shifts)

Events & Outreach

Help spread the word about KNFC. Tasks include:

  • Tabling at community events
  • Helping with in-store events
  • Distributing flyers
  • Social media assistance

Time commitment: Flexible/as needed

Best for: Social, enthusiastic people

Cleaning & Maintenance

Help keep our store clean and welcoming. Tasks include:

  • Cleaning floors and surfaces
  • Organizing storage areas
  • Light maintenance tasks
  • Recycling and composting

Time commitment: [X-X hours/week]

Best for: Those who take pride in cleanliness

How to Get Started

Step 1: Contact Us

Reach out to our Volunteer Coordinator to express your interest and discuss available opportunities.

Step 2: Application

Complete a volunteer application form. This helps us learn about your interests and availability.

Step 3: Orientation

Attend a brief orientation to learn about the co-op, food safety, and your volunteer role.

Step 4: Start Volunteering!

Begin your shifts and start making a difference in your community!
